Letter names and shortcuts

Any listed name spelling is accepted. Shortcuts are the short codes after each name; edit them to suit yourself (separate several with commas). Names like vet, khaf and fe count for their letter. Shin and Sin are separate cards, and their dot is always shown, even with vowel points off. For words, type the letters in reading order: the rightmost Hebrew letter first. Separate letters with spaces, or run shortcuts together if you like (bayz is fine only when it can be read one way).
